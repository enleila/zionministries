Partners Group

Joining a Partners Group allows you to grow spiritually, build strong relationships, and serve alongside others in a supportive environment. Each group provides mentorship, Bible study, and practical guidance to help members deepen their faith, discover their gifts, and actively participate in the ministry’s mission of impacting lives and communities.

Our Partners Groups foster fellowship, accountability, and encouragement, connecting like-minded believers to pray, share, and collaborate. By joining, you gain a sense of belonging, opportunities to serve in various ministries, and the chance to develop leadership skills while growing closer to God and making meaningful contributions within the church family.
