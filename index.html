Shape Your Life with the Words of Life.

Psalm 103:8 NKJV says, “The Lord is merciful and gracious, Slow to anger, and abounding in mercy.”

God is reminding you today of His true character. He is merciful. That means He does not treat us as our sins deserve. He is gracious. That means He gives us blessings we do not deserve. He is slow to anger. God does not react quickly in judgment. He is patient. He gives time for repentance. He is abounding in mercy. His mercy is not small or limited. It is great, deep, and overflowing. Mercy is not something God has sometimes. Mercy is who He is.
