Atapara Crusade

The Zion Anniversary is more than a date on the calendar — it is a joyful celebration of God’s faithfulness, growth, and the many lives transformed through the ministry. Held at the church grounds, this special occasion is filled with vibrant praise and worship, heartfelt thanksgiving, testimonies, music, and a spirit of unity that brings the entire congregation together in cheerful celebration.

But our anniversary is not only about celebration — it is also about compassion. As part of this meaningful event, we extend love beyond the church walls by giving back to the community. We reach out to widows, vulnerable families, and those in need through acts of generosity, support, and encouragement. It is our way of living out the gospel — not just in word, but in action.

A beautiful reminder that we are blessed to be a blessing. As we celebrate together on the church grounds, we also sow seeds of hope into society, sharing joy, love, and practical care with those around us. It is a celebration of faith, family, and a mission that continues to shine brightly.
