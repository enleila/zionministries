Join the Team

Becoming a minister is a calling to serve God and His people, guiding them spiritually, offering counsel, and leading by example. It requires dedication, humility, and a heart surrendered to God’s purpose.

A minister grows through prayer, study of Scripture, and active service within the church community. This path shapes leaders who impact lives, nurture faith, and help others walk in God’s calling with integrity and compassion.


With Us Through the Following Ministries

Ushering & Security.

Our Ushering & Security team warmly welcomes congregants, ensures safety, guides seating, and maintains order during every service and event.

Kids Church.

Kids Church nurtures children spiritually, teaching Bible stories, songs, and values, creating a fun, safe environment for faith growth.

Music Team.

The Music Team leads worship, inspires the congregation with praise, and creates an atmosphere where hearts connect deeply with God.

Prayer Team.

Our Prayer Team intercedes for members, supports spiritual needs, and stands in faith, trusting God to bring breakthrough and healing.

Administrative Support.

Administrative Support organizes church operations, manages records, coordinates schedules, and ensures smooth functioning of programs and events daily.

Production Design.

Production Design creates visuals, stage setups, and presentations, enhancing worship experiences and making events engaging and memorable for all.

Technical & Stage Management.

Technical & Stage Management operates sound, lighting, and multimedia, coordinating stage activities to ensure every service runs seamlessly and professionally.

Communications.

Communications manages announcements, social media, and outreach, keeping members informed and connected while promoting the ministry to the wider community.
